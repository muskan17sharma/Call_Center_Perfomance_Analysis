# 📞 Call Center Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Call Center Performance Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to evaluate call center operations, agent performance, customer satisfaction, and issue resolution efficiency.

The dashboard transforms call-level data into actionable business insights by tracking key performance indicators such as **Total Calls, Answered Calls, Answer Rate, Resolution Rate, Average Speed of Answer, and Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)**.

The project helps management identify operational gaps, monitor agent performance, understand customer concerns, and improve the overall customer service experience.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze call center performance and answer key business questions:

* How many customer calls are being received and answered?
* What percentage of calls are successfully answered?
* How effectively are customer issues being resolved?
* How quickly are customers connected with agents?
* How satisfied are customers with the service?
* Which agents are performing better?
* Which customer issues/topics generate the highest call volume?
* Is successful issue resolution translating into a positive customer experience?
* How can call center efficiency and customer satisfaction be improved?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### KPI Overview

The dashboard provides a high-level view of the most important call center metrics:

* Total Calls
* Answered Calls
* Answer Rate %
* Resolved Rate %
* Average Speed of Answer
* Average Satisfaction Rating

These KPIs allow management to quickly understand the overall operational health of the call center.

### Monthly Call Trend

A monthly trend analysis is included to monitor changes in call volume over time and identify periods of increasing or decreasing customer demand.

### Answered vs Unanswered Calls

The dashboard compares answered and unanswered calls to evaluate call-handling effectiveness and identify potential gaps in agent availability or staffing.

### Agent Performance Analysis

Agent-level performance is evaluated using:

* Total Calls
* Answer Rate
* Resolved Rate
* Customer Satisfaction

This enables performance comparison across agents and helps identify opportunities for coaching and improvement.

### Call Topic Analysis

Customer issues are categorized by topic to identify the areas generating the highest volume of support requests.

This can help management prioritize resources, improve self-service options, and identify recurring customer problems.

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

* Date
* Agent
* Topic
* Satisfaction Rating

These filters allow users to drill down into specific segments and perform targeted analysis.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Strong Issue Resolution

The call center achieves a **90% resolution rate**, indicating that the majority of customer issues are successfully addressed.

### 2. Customer Satisfaction Needs Improvement

Despite the high resolution rate, the average customer satisfaction score is **3.40/5**, which is below the desired **4.0+ target**.

This indicates that successfully resolving an issue does not necessarily guarantee a positive customer experience.

### 3. High Waiting Time

The **Average Speed of Answer is 67.52 seconds**, significantly higher than the recommended target of **30 seconds or less**.

Long waiting times can negatively impact customer satisfaction even when the customer's issue is eventually resolved.

### 4. Resolution ≠ Customer Experience

The combination of a **90% resolution rate** and **3.40/5 CSAT** highlights an important operational gap: the quality and speed of the service experience matter in addition to simply resolving the customer's problem.

---

## 🔎 Root Cause Analysis

The relatively low customer satisfaction may be influenced by:

* Long customer waiting times
* Delays before connecting with an agent
* Uneven workload distribution
* Agent availability during high-volume periods
* Inefficient call routing
* Customer experience issues during the interaction

The dashboard therefore focuses not only on resolution performance but also on the customer journey leading up to resolution.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

### Reduce Average Speed of Answer

Target an **ASA of 30 seconds or less** by:

* Optimizing agent staffing
* Improving shift planning
* Monitoring peak call periods
* Optimizing call routing
* Balancing workloads across agents

### Improve Customer Satisfaction

Work toward increasing **CSAT from 3.40/5 to 4.0+** by:

* Reducing waiting time
* Improving agent communication
* Identifying recurring customer complaints
* Providing targeted agent training
* Monitoring low-satisfaction interactions

### Monitor Agent Performance

Use agent-level KPIs to identify:

* High-performing agents
* Agents requiring additional training
* Differences in resolution efficiency
* Satisfaction gaps between agents

### Analyze High-Volume Topics

Focus on frequently occurring customer issues and explore:

* Better FAQs
* Self-service solutions
* Knowledge-base improvements
* Process improvements
* Automated support options

## 📚 Project Summary

This project demonstrates how **Power BI, DAX, and data visualization** can be used to convert raw call center data into a management-focused analytical solution.

The key business finding is that while the call center demonstrates **strong issue resolution performance (90%)**, there is an opportunity to improve the overall customer experience by reducing the **67.52-second average answer time** and increasing **customer satisfaction from 3.40/5 toward the 4.0+ target**.

The dashboard therefore provides both **performance monitoring and actionable recommendations** to support better call center operations and customer experience.
